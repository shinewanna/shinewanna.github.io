Your Privacy, Clearly Explained

Text Hider does not collect personal data directly. We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to improve app stability and experience. All your hidden texts and encryption settings stay entirely on your device.

01 — Overview

What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Text Hider handles your information. Text Hider is a local Android app for encrypting and hiding text. Your encrypted texts, keys, and settings are stored locally on your device and never transmitted. However, we do use Firebase services (Analytics and Crashlytics) to help improve app stability and user experience.

02 — Data Collection

What We Do & Don't Collect

Text Hider does not directly collect any of the following:

✕ Personal information
✕ Usage data or analytics
✕ Device identifiers
✕ IP addresses
✕ Location data
✕ Contacts or messages
✕ Photos or media
✕ App usage statistics
Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may collect anonymous technical data such as device model, OS version, and crash logs. This is used solely to improve app performance and fix bugs. It cannot be used to identify you personally.
03 — Permissions

Why We Request Permissions

Text Hider requests only the permissions necessary to function. No permission is used to collect or transmit data.

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Required for Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics reporting
04 — Third Parties

Third Party Services

Text Hider does not use any advertising SDKs or cloud sync services. Specifically, we do not use:

  • Advertising SDKs — the app is 100% ad-free
  • Cloud storage or sync services
Firebase (Google) We use Firebase services provided by Google LLC for the following purposes:

Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous usage data such as screen views and app events to help us understand how the app is used and improve the user experience. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Firebase Crashlytics — automatically collects crash reports when the app encounters an error. This helps us identify and fix bugs quickly. Crash reports may include device model, OS version, and app version at the time of the crash.

Firebase is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at policies.google.com/privacy
05 — Local Storage

All Data Stays On Your Device

The app stores your encrypted texts and settings locally on your device. Nothing is ever sent to any server.

This local data is entirely under your control. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from your device. We have no copy of it and no way to access it.
06 — Security

Your Texts Are Private

All your encrypted texts, keys, and settings are stored and processed entirely on your device. They are never transmitted to any server or shared with anyone. Only you have access to your hidden texts.

07 — Children

Safe for All Ages

Since Text Hider does not collect personal data, there is no risk of collecting information from users of any age, including children under 13. The app is safe for all users.

08 — Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
